# CRM Selection Criteria: Speed, Cost, Customization, Reporting, and Adoption

CRM decisions are easier when the team agrees on the criteria before comparing vendors.

Most CRM tradeoffs fall into five categories: speed, cost, customization, reporting, and adoption.

## Speed

Some teams need a CRM they can launch quickly. Speed matters when the current process is broken, leads are being missed, or the team needs a shared system immediately.

## Cost

Cost is not only the monthly subscription price. It also includes setup time, training, integrations, add-ons, and the internal effort needed to maintain the system.

## Customization

Customization matters when the sales process is complex, the team has multiple pipelines, or the business needs custom fields, workflows, permissions, or reporting structures.

## Reporting

Reporting matters when leadership needs visibility into pipeline, lead quality, marketing source, sales activity, and revenue outcomes.

## Adoption

Adoption may be the most important criterion. A powerful CRM does not help if the team avoids using it.

## Bottom line

The best CRM is not simply the cheapest, fastest, or most customizable option. It is the option that best matches the team’s real priorities and operating constraints.
